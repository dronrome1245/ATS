# ATS Research Review Decisions

**Part of:** `ATS2-M1-010` / `RESEARCH_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md`

## Roles

- **Preparer** assembles the package and requested decision.
- **Reviewer** checks completeness, lineage, evidence class/sufficiency and negative evidence.
- **Decision authority** issues the decision allowed by the current stage/governance.

Early research roles may overlap when no financial exposure is created and the overlap is recorded. Any stage requiring formal independence uses that stronger rule. OWNER-only decisions remain OWNER-only.

## Stage-appropriate outcomes

Research Review does not invent one universal enum. It applies the decision model defined by the governing stage, including as applicable:

`SCREEN / PARK / REJECT`

`DATA_FEASIBILITY / PARK / REJECT`

`FEASIBLE / CONDITIONAL / PARK / REJECT`

`ADVANCE_TO_ECONOMIC_SCREEN / ITERATE / PARK / REJECT`

`ADVANCE_TO_CANDIDATE_REVIEW / RETURN_TO_RESEARCH / PARK / REJECT`

`PROMOTE / PARK / REJECT`

The review names the methodology that supplied its decision model.

## Positive-decision rule

Positive progression requires: non-blocking input completeness, valid lineage/current context, required evidence class, stage-sufficient evidence, no fatal integrity defect, all stage prerequisites and a reason based on pre-existing evidence/criteria.

A favorable headline metric cannot override a blocking failure.

## Bounded return to research

Any `RETURN_TO_RESEARCH`, `ITERATE` or equivalent must state: exact unresolved question, why it matters, allowed next action, expected artifact, what resolves the question, what material change requires a new entity/Experiment, and a stop condition.

Repeated returns without new rationale/evidence are grounds to `PARK` or `REJECT`, not unlimited rescue work.

## Negative evidence and dissent

Material negative/failed evidence, discarded alternatives, contradictions and reviewer dissent remain in the record. Dissent does not force rejection, but it cannot be deleted to manufacture consensus.

## Immutable record and re-review

Issued reviews are historical. New evidence or material change creates a new Review ID linked to the prior review. The old review is not rewritten.
