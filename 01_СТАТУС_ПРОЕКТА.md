Dukascopy structurally provides what HYP-0001 needs:

- EUR/USD;
- 1 Hour historical bars;
- separate BID and ASK sides;
- historical export / JForex history access;
- a clean distinction between incomplete current candle and latest fully formed candle.

For PROTO-01, UTC is the canonical research timezone. BID and ASK timestamps must align exactly. Missing bars are not filled. Any scheduled weekend/session closure breaks the 24-bar contiguous window.
