## Statement

For every eligible scheduled U.S. Consumer Price Index release event with public release timestamp `t`, represent synchronized EUR/USD BID/ASK information at three boundaries under a lag/tolerance rule that Data Feasibility must freeze before outcome calculation:

- `t - 60 minutes`;
- `t`;
- `t + 60 minutes`.

Define the mid price at each boundary as:

`mid_x = (bid_x + ask_x) / 2`.

Define:

`pre_abs_60m_return = abs(mid_t / mid_{t-60m} - 1)`

`post_abs_60m_return = abs(mid_{t+60m} / mid_t - 1)`.

The primary HYP-0006 claim is:

`median(post_abs_60m_return) > median(pre_abs_60m_return)`

across the complete frozen inventory of eligible U.S. CPI release events.

The comparison is paired by event inventory but the primary statistic is the ordering of the two full event distributions. No directional EUR/USD return sign is predicted.

Quoted spread around `t`, event count, calendar/regime concentration and coincident-release flags are required diagnostics but do not replace the primary inequality.

## Mechanism

The proposed mechanism is scheduled information arrival and rapid FX price discovery.

A U.S. CPI publication can update inflation and monetary-policy expectations at a known release event, producing more immediate price adjustment after public information arrives than during the directly preceding hour.

The mechanism is structural rather than directional. A larger post-release absolute move does not itself imply a profitable trading rule, and the line may later fail on spread, execution, concentration or economic grounds even if the structural inequality holds.

## Falsification

HYP-0006 receives evidence against the primary claim if any of the following holds:

1. `median(post_abs_60m_return) <= median(pre_abs_60m_return)`;
2. the claimed ordering requires changing the event category, instrument or 60-minute horizons after outcome inspection;
3. the result requires selecting favorable events, years, regimes or coincident-release subsets after inspection;
4. the public-release timestamp cannot be represented honestly enough to anchor the three boundaries;
5. the effect appears only after using incoherent BID/ASK sources, stale replacement quotes or an unfrozen boundary-lag rule;
6. the test requires HYP-0005 frozen unseen data for tuning or outcome computation;
7. Data Feasibility cannot establish a reproducible event inventory and granular quote path with explicit timing semantics.

A favorable spread diagnostic or a favorable subset cannot rescue failure of the primary inequality.
