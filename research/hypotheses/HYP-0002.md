## Statement

For EUR/USD spot FX, define:

`R24(t) = MID_CLOSE(t) / MID_CLOSE(t-24) - 1`

with `MID_CLOSE = (BID_CLOSE + ASK_CLOSE) / 2` and a contiguous 24-bar lookback.

Using only the already development-known 2021–2025 block, Data Feasibility must deterministically calculate and freeze:

- `Q10_DEV` = 10th percentile of eligible `R24`;
- `Q90_DEV` = 90th percentile of eligible `R24`.

Those numeric thresholds must be fixed before any new confirmatory block is inspected and must not be recalculated from that block.

On a future previously unseen eligible block:

1. **High-tail / DOWN reversal:** when `R24(t) >= Q90_DEV`, the signed DOWN return from the next eligible BID open to the ASK open 24 eligible 1H bars later must have mean `> 0` and mean `>` the unconditional signed DOWN 24H baseline.
2. **Low-tail / UP reversal:** when `R24(t) <= Q10_DEV`, the signed UP return from the next eligible ASK open to the BID open 24 eligible 1H bars later must have mean `> 0` and mean `>` the unconditional signed UP 24H baseline.

The primary HYP-0002 claim is two-sided: both tails must satisfy both inequalities. Dropping one side after seeing a result is a material reformulation requiring a new Hypothesis ID.

## Mechanism

The proposed mechanism is temporary overshoot followed by partial price reversal.

An unusually large multi-hour move may reflect short-lived order-flow imbalance, temporary liquidity depletion, position crowding, profit-taking pressure or later rebalancing. Once the initiating pressure fades, price may partially reverse.

The mechanism is uncertain. The favorable development discovery is provenance for asking the question, not proof that the effect persists on unseen data.

## Falsification

HYP-0002 receives evidence against its primary claim if:

1. high-tail/DOWN mean signed return is `<= 0`; or
2. high-tail/DOWN mean is `<=` its unconditional DOWN baseline; or
3. low-tail/UP mean signed return is `<= 0`; or
4. low-tail/UP mean is `<=` its unconditional UP baseline; or
5. the effect requires look-ahead, same-close execution, confirmatory-block threshold calibration or inconsistent BID/ASK treatment; or
6. unseen data are too sparse, dependent, incomplete or temporally ambiguous to answer the claim honestly.

If only one side survives, HYP-0002 is not rewritten into a one-sided winner.
