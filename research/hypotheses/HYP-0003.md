## Research origin and development role

HYP-0003 is a **development-stage** Hypothesis.

It does not freeze one supposedly optimal momentum parameter set. Instead, it asks whether a predeclared literature-motivated parameter grid contains a **broad positive continuation region** rather than one isolated favorable cell.

The grid is fixed before ATS evaluates Strategy #3 development data.

The declared formation periods `1, 3, 6, 9, 12` months and holding periods `1, 3, 6, 9, 12` months are fixed as the development search space.

Literature provenance:

- Menkhoff, Sarno, Schmeling & Schrimpf, *Currency Momentum Strategies*, BIS Working Paper 366.
- The paper evaluates formation and holding periods `1, 3, 6, 9, 12` months and sorts currencies cross-sectionally by lagged returns.
- The same literature reports material sensitivity to transaction costs.

These published choices motivate the bounded grid. Published profitability is not ATS evidence.

## Statement

For a Data-Feasibility-approved FX currency universe with at least six eligible currencies at each monthly decision point:

1. orient each currency against a common USD numeraire so a positive normalized return always means that currency appreciated versus USD;
2. calculate each currency's completed past spot return over formation period
   `f ∈ {1, 3, 6, 9, 12} months`;
3. rank eligible currencies cross-sectionally using only information available at the decision timestamp;
4. assign the strongest one-sixth to a winner basket and the weakest one-sixth to a loser basket;
5. measure an equal-weight executable winner-minus-loser spot return over holding period
   `h ∈ {1, 3, 6, 9, 12} months`;
6. use side-aware BID/ASK entry and exit prices no earlier than the first eligible quote after the ranking timestamp.

This creates exactly `25` predeclared `(f, h)` development variants.

The primary HYP-0003 claim is that cross-sectional currency momentum appears as a **broad development region**, defined before results by all three conditions:

- the median of the `25` variant mean winner-minus-loser returns is `> 0`;
- at least `13 / 25` variant mean winner-minus-loser returns are `> 0`;
- the positive-mean variants span at least `3 / 5` distinct formation periods and at least `3 / 5` distinct holding periods.

The economic label for this development screen is:

`QUOTE_EXECUTABLE_AFTER_SPREAD_BEFORE_FINANCING`

Financing, rollover, commission and other applicable costs must be assessed explicitly before later economic interpretation.

## Why HYP-0003 is not the final confirmatory Hypothesis

HYP-0003 answers:

> Is there a reasonably broad development region worth freezing into a separate confirmatory claim?

It does **not** answer:

> Which parameter combination is proven profitable?

If HYP-0003 survives its development screen, ATS must create a **new Hypothesis ID** for the fixed confirmatory formulation. That later Hypothesis must freeze one parameter rule/formulation and a separate previously unseen confirmatory block before result observation.

Development results from HYP-0003 may motivate that new Hypothesis, but they cannot themselves be relabeled as confirmatory evidence.

## Mechanism

The proposed mechanism is persistence in relative currency performance.

Delayed information incorporation, gradual portfolio adjustment, investor under-reaction, persistent positioning or slow-moving capital may allow currencies with stronger recent relative performance to continue outperforming weaker currencies for some later interval.

The mechanism is uncertain.

The same mechanism can be overwhelmed by turnover, spread, financing, unstable regimes or rapid arbitrage, so positive gross ranking behavior is not assumed to be economically exploitable.

## Conditions

The claim applies only when:

1. the same explicit universe-eligibility rule is used for all `25` variants;
2. eligibility is based on point-in-time data availability/executability, not future performance;
3. each ranking uses only completed information available by the monthly decision timestamp;
4. all currencies are normalized to the same appreciation-sign convention versus USD;
5. BID/ASK side semantics are explicit for entry and exit;
6. missing/stale/asynchronous observations are handled by a rule frozen before development results;
7. no variant is omitted because its development result is unattractive;
8. all `25 / 25` declared variants are retained in the result;
9. no future confirmatory data influence the development grid or breadth rule.

The Hypothesis makes no claim when fewer than six currencies satisfy the frozen eligibility rule at a decision point.

## Expected direction

For a genuine cross-sectional continuation effect:

- stronger past currencies should have higher subsequent signed executable spot returns than weaker past currencies;
- therefore the equal-weight winner-minus-loser mean return should be positive across a broad portion of the declared grid.

No target return magnitude is assumed.

## Decision-time information premise

At each monthly ranking timestamp, the system may use only:

- completed historical quotes/bars through the frozen decision cutoff;
- static metadata known by that time;
- the frozen universe-eligibility rule;
- the frozen return-orientation and ranking rules.

It may not use future quotes, future universe membership, a later data-quality label, future normalization statistics or any parameter choice learned from confirmatory data.

Entry measurement occurs no earlier than the first eligible quote after the completed ranking timestamp.

## Falsification

HYP-0003 receives evidence against its primary development claim if any of the following holds:

1. the median of the `25` variant mean winner-minus-loser returns is `<= 0`; or
2. fewer than `13 / 25` variant mean returns are `> 0`; or
3. positive-mean variants span fewer than three formation periods; or
4. positive-mean variants span fewer than three holding periods; or
5. the apparent effect requires performance-selected universe membership, look-ahead, future-known normalization, same-timestamp execution unavailable in practice, or inconsistent BID/ASK treatment; or
6. available data cannot produce a stable minimum six-currency point-in-time cross-section; or
7. the result can only be presented by hiding unattractive cells from the declared `25`-cell grid.

A single highly profitable development cell does not rescue HYP-0003 if the breadth criteria fail.
