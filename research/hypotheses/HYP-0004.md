## Research role

HYP-0004 is a bounded structural Hypothesis for Strategy #4.

It does not define a directional trading rule and does not claim profitability. It asks whether a predeclared fixed-UTC time-of-day contrast produces a reproducible difference in subsequent EUR/USD movement while not being explained by wider quoted spread.

The clock windows are fixed before ATS computes any HYP-0004 outcome.

## Statement

For `EURUSD` spot FX observations represented in UTC, classify each eligible hourly decision boundary `t` by its UTC hour:

- `CORE_ACTIVE`: hour `07` through hour `16` inclusive;
- `TRANSITION`: hour `17` through hour `20` inclusive;
- `OFF_HOURS`: hour `21`, `22`, `23`, or hour `00` through hour `06` inclusive.

For every eligible decision boundary, use synchronized point-in-time BID and ASK information available at or immediately after `t`, under a lag/continuity rule frozen by Data Feasibility.

Define:

- `mid_t = (bid_t + ask_t) / 2`;
- `relative_spread_t = (ask_t - bid_t) / mid_t`;
- `abs_next_1h_return_t = abs(mid_{t+1h} / mid_t - 1)`, where the future boundary is exactly one hour later under the frozen continuity rule.

The primary HYP-0004 claim is that both conditions hold on the frozen development dataset:

1. the median `abs_next_1h_return` for `CORE_ACTIVE` observations is greater than the median for `OFF_HOURS` observations; and
2. the median `relative_spread` for `CORE_ACTIVE` observations is less than or equal to the median for `OFF_HOURS` observations.

`TRANSITION` observations are retained and reported but do not enter the primary two-window decision rule.

No minimum return magnitude, Sharpe ratio or trading P&L is claimed.

## Mechanism

The proposed mechanism is recurring variation in global FX participation, liquidity and information processing over the 24-hour trading day.

European and North-American participation should create a higher-activity portion of the UTC day, while transatlantic off-hours should generally contain less EUR/USD price movement and no tighter liquidity advantage.

The mechanism is structural rather than directional. A larger absolute move is not itself a profitable strategy and can be economically irrelevant if spread, slippage or later execution assumptions dominate.

The mechanism is uncertain and may fail because market structure changes, activity shifts across regimes, or the selected fixed-UTC contrast is not stable.

## Conditions

The claim applies only when:

1. observation timestamps have unambiguous UTC semantics;
2. BID and ASK at each decision boundary are from one coherent source representation;
3. the decision-boundary lag rule is frozen before the HYP-0004 outcome is computed;
4. the next outcome boundary is exactly one hour later under the frozen continuity rule;
5. weekend, holiday and market-closure gaps are not bridged as if they were continuous one-hour observations;
6. missing or malformed quote pairs are handled by a rule frozen before outcome inspection;
7. all eligible observations are retained regardless of whether their outcomes support the claim;
8. the three UTC buckets remain exactly as declared above;
9. no alternative clock window is searched inside HYP-0004 after the result is known.

The claim intentionally uses fixed UTC clock time. It does not relabel the buckets as London, New York or Asia sessions and does not shift the primary windows with daylight-saving time.

## Expected direction

The expected form is a two-part structural ordering:

- `median(abs_next_1h_return | CORE_ACTIVE) > median(abs_next_1h_return | OFF_HOURS)`;
- `median(relative_spread | CORE_ACTIVE) <= median(relative_spread | OFF_HOURS)`.

Both conditions are required for the primary HYP-0004 development claim.

No directional EUR/USD return sign is predicted.

## Decision-time information premise

At decision boundary `t`, the research process may use only:

- the UTC timestamp;
- synchronized BID/ASK information available at or after the boundary under the frozen lag rule;
- static source metadata known by that time;
- the predeclared clock-bucket mapping.

The next-hour quote is an outcome and is not available at the decision timestamp.

The research process may not use future quotes to choose the bucket, alter the boundary lag, remove unattractive dates, redefine weekdays, or select an alternative window after observing results.

## Falsification

HYP-0004 receives evidence against its primary claim if any of the following holds:

1. `median(abs_next_1h_return | CORE_ACTIVE) <= median(abs_next_1h_return | OFF_HOURS)`; or
2. `median(relative_spread | CORE_ACTIVE) > median(relative_spread | OFF_HOURS)`; or
3. the apparent contrast requires changing the declared UTC windows after outcome inspection; or
4. the result depends on bridging non-contiguous market gaps, using incoherent BID/ASK sources, or future-known timestamp corrections; or
5. Data Feasibility cannot represent synchronized boundary quotes and exact one-hour outcomes with sufficiently clear timing semantics to test the claim honestly.

A favorable `TRANSITION` bucket does not rescue the primary claim if either primary inequality fails.

## Known biases and leakage risks

- choosing UTC windows after inspecting historical HYP-0004 outcomes;
- changing fixed UTC windows to local-session labels after the fact;
- daylight-saving interpretation drift;
- weekend or holiday gap bridging;
- multiple testing across alternative hour buckets;
- selecting only favorable weekdays or calendar periods;
- timestamp ambiguity between bar open, close and vendor receive time;
- separately sourced or incoherent BID/ASK observations;
- stale quotes around low-liquidity periods;
- spread changes that make larger price movement economically irrelevant;
- regime dependence and concentration in a few years;
- dependence created by overlapping outcomes if the exact one-hour rule is not respected;
- reusing a later revealed block to redefine the same HYP-0004 clock windows.

7. if both pass, retain the result as development evidence only and decide separately whether a later fixed trading formulation or confirmatory test is justified.

No trading P&L or profitability claim is required for this cheap structural test.
