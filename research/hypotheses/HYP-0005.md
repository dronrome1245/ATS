## Statement

On a future Data-Feasibility-approved EUR/USD confirmatory dataset that is disjoint from the HYP-0004 development evidence, classify every eligible hourly UTC boundary `t` using the same fixed buckets:

- `CORE_ACTIVE`: UTC hours `07` through `16` inclusive;
- `TRANSITION`: UTC hours `17` through `20` inclusive;
- `OFF_HOURS`: UTC hours `21`, `22`, `23`, and `00` through `06` inclusive.

For every eligible boundary:

`mid_t = (bid_t + ask_t) / 2`

`relative_spread_t = (ask_t - bid_t) / mid_t`

`abs_next_1h_return_t = abs(mid_{t+1h} / mid_t - 1)`

The HYP-0005 confirmatory claim requires BOTH:

1. `median(abs_next_1h_return | CORE_ACTIVE) > median(abs_next_1h_return | OFF_HOURS)`;
2. `median(relative_spread | CORE_ACTIVE) <= median(relative_spread | OFF_HOURS)`.

`TRANSITION` remains reporting-only and cannot affect the two primary inequalities.

No minimum effect-size threshold is added after the HYP-0004 reveal.

## Mechanism

The inherited mechanism is recurring variation in global FX participation, liquidity and information processing across the 24-hour trading day.

The confirmatory question is whether the same fixed-UTC structural ordering appears on an unseen data block rather than being specific to the HYP-0004 development period.

The mechanism remains structural and non-directional. Larger absolute movement does not itself imply a profitable trading rule.

## Conditions

The claim applies only when:

1. timestamps have unambiguous UTC semantics;
2. BID and ASK come from one coherent source representation;
3. every observation is assigned to the fixed UTC bucket before outcome calculation;
4. the future boundary is exactly `t + 1 hour` under the frozen continuity rule;
5. weekend, holiday and market-closure gaps are not bridged;
6. missing/malformed observations follow a rule frozen before confirmatory outcome inspection;
7. all eligible observations are retained regardless of result;
8. the confirmatory evidence block is disjoint from the HYP-0004 development block;
9. the exact confirmatory coverage and source snapshot are frozen by Data Feasibility before any HYP-0005 outcome is computed;
10. no alternate UTC windows, weekday subset, lag, horizon, instrument or favorable-year subset is searched after reveal.

The fixed UTC buckets are not relabeled or shifted for daylight-saving time.

## Falsification

HYP-0005 receives evidence against the confirmatory claim if any of the following occurs:

1. `median(abs_next_1h_return | CORE_ACTIVE) <= median(abs_next_1h_return | OFF_HOURS)`;
2. `median(relative_spread | CORE_ACTIVE) > median(relative_spread | OFF_HOURS)`;
3. the result requires changing the fixed UTC buckets, one-hour horizon, instrument or primary inequalities;
4. the result requires selecting favorable weekdays, years or subperiods after inspection;
5. continuity requires bridging missing/closed hours;
6. the purported confirmatory block is not demonstrably separate from consumed development evidence;
7. Data Feasibility cannot establish sufficiently clear point-in-time BID/ASK and timing semantics.

A favorable `TRANSITION` result cannot rescue failure of either primary inequality.
