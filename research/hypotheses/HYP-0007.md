## Statement

On **one future Data-Feasibility-certified, frozen, genuinely unseen**
U.S. CPI event block that is separate from HYP-0006's development events
and has not been used by another ATS line for result-driven selection,
represent each eligible official release timestamp `t` by exactly
three synchronized same-record EURUSD BID/ASK anchors:

1. `PRE_60_ANCHOR`: nearest quote timestamp to `t - 60m` within
   maximum 30,000 ms absolute gap, tie chooses previous.
2. `PRE_RELEASE_ANCHOR`: last quote **strictly before t** within
   maximum 30,000 ms staleness.
3. `POST_60_ANCHOR`: nearest quote timestamp to `t + 60m` within
   maximum 30,000 ms absolute gap, tie chooses previous.

For any duplicated chosen millisecond, choose the last original raw
record and obtain BID and ASK from that **same** record; no interpolation
or synthetic spread repair.

The inherited equations are:

`mid = (bid + ask) / 2`

`pre_abs_60m_return = abs(mid_pre_release / mid_pre_60 - 1)`

`post_abs_60m_return = abs(mid_post_60 / mid_pre_release - 1)`

The only primary claim is:

`median(post_abs_60m_return) > median(pre_abs_60m_return)`

over **all eligible confirmatory events in one future pre-outcome-frozen
inventory**. Sample size, source snapshot, inclusion/missing-data policy
and exact time interval are **NOT YET SELECTED**; Data Feasibility must
approve their honesty and independence *before* prices/outcomes are seen.
The old 216-event HYP-0006 denominator cannot be reused as confirmatory.

This is a structural, nondirectional claim and never a directional
trade or profitability prediction.
