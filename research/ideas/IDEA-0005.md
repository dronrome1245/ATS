## Observation

Scheduled macroeconomic releases create discrete information-arrival times at which foreign-exchange prices may reprice more sharply than immediately before the release.

For this new line, ATS narrows the previously retained macro-announcement family to scheduled U.S. Consumer Price Index releases and an initial EUR/USD structural-response question.

No ATS CPI-event outcome has been measured for this Idea.

## Preliminary rationale

The proposed mechanism is scheduled information arrival and immediate price discovery.

A CPI release can alter expectations about inflation, monetary policy and relative rates, creating a short-lived increase in price adjustment around the publication time. The first ATS question is deliberately structural and non-directional: does the immediate absolute EUR/USD move become larger after the release than immediately before it?

This does not assert a tradable directional edge. Wider spreads, coincident releases, timestamp uncertainty, dependence across events or weak sample breadth may make the effect unusable or untestable.

## Expected effect

- Direction/type of effect: `EVENT_CONDITIONAL_ABSOLUTE_RETURN_SHIFT`
- Expected observable consequence: across a predeclared inventory of eligible U.S. CPI releases, the immediate post-release absolute EUR/USD movement may exceed the immediately preceding movement under one fixed event-window definition.

No directional EUR/USD sign, surprise threshold, consensus input, alternate event category or profitability threshold is selected by this Idea.

## Cheapest useful test

After HYP-0006 and Data Feasibility freeze one event inventory, quote source, period, timestamp rule and event-window geometry:

1. retain every eligible U.S. CPI release in the frozen period;
2. measure one fixed absolute EUR/USD return immediately before the release and one fixed absolute return immediately after it;
3. compare the two predeclared distributions with one primary ordering;
4. report event counts, calendar/regime concentration, coincident-release flags and quoted spread diagnostics;
5. do not search alternative event categories, horizons or directional signs inside the same Hypothesis after the result is known;
6. stop or create a new research attempt if the primary ordering fails.

The first test remains structural. It does not calculate trading P&L or claim profitability.

## Known risks of false inference

- scheduled time differs from actual public availability time;
- historical release times or reschedules are represented incorrectly;
- daylight-saving conversion errors;
- coincident macro releases confound attribution to CPI alone;
- sparse monthly events and regime concentration;
- multiple testing across event categories or event-window horizons;
- choosing EUR/USD subperiods after outcome inspection;
- spread widening or stale quotes around release time;
- using bar timestamps that do not represent the release boundary honestly;
- dependence from overlapping or clustered events;
- reusing previously inspected ATS quote periods and mislabeling them independent;
- using either frozen HYP-0005 unseen segment to tune or evaluate HYP-0006 while HYP-0005 remains unrevealed.
