## Observation

In the bounded 16-alternative EURUSD H1 search, none of eight continuation alternatives met the shortlist rule, while five of eight reversal alternatives did. All four reversal variants with a 24H effect horizon were shortlisted.

The Idea carries forward only the broad reversal family, not a parameter winner.

## Preliminary rationale

A plausible mechanism is temporary overshoot after an unusually large multi-hour move. Temporary order-flow imbalance, liquidity depletion, profit-taking and position rebalancing can in principle produce a later partial reversal.

This mechanism is uncertain and is distinct from the observed discovery result.

## Expected effect

- Direction/type of effect: `TWO_SIDED_REVERSAL_AFTER_EXTREME_PRIOR_RETURN`
- Expected observable consequence: after a predeclared extreme positive prior move, a signed DOWN return may exceed its unconditional same-direction baseline; after an extreme negative prior move, a signed UP return may do the same.

No exact threshold, lookback, horizon or target magnitude is fixed by this Idea.

- approximately `84%–90%` overlap for shortlisted 24H variants;
- repeated use of the already development-known 2021–2025 block;
- spread-only execution approximation; commission and financing excluded;
- possible tail/outlier sensitivity;
- look-ahead or same-close execution;
